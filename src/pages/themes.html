Themes

Three palettes, and the system's answer as a fourth choice. The same project, the same file, the same window size — so what differs between these pictures is only the colour.

⌘, → Editor → Appearance. It takes effect on the windows that are already open: the palette is swapped underneath them rather than applied to the next one.

Every picture on this page was taken by make theme-shots, which runs the app once per theme with --theme and captures the window. The colours listed under each are read out of the app's own Theme.swift, so what is named here is what is drawn.

Abydos

Settings: Abydos Terminal scheme: abydos The default for the pictures on this site

The app's own palette: a sun on the horizon, which is what the icon is. Amber earns its place three times over — it is CRT phosphor, it is desert, and it is a warm ground in a category that is wall to wall blue. The surfaces are nearly black with warmth in them rather than brown: a brown editor is tiring within the hour, and what should be warm is the light falling on it, not the paper.

  • #1C1712 sidebar
  • #E8D9C0 text
  • #FFE0AC core light
  • #F7B44E amber

Dusk

Settings: Dark What System resolves to after dark

The dark theme, for anyone who wants the familiar arrangement: quiet chrome, one warm colour for keywords, green for the text you wrote, blue for the things that are called. The greys carry a little more blue than most, and the syntax hues are pulled apart so keyword, type and call read as three colours rather than as three shades of one.

  • #24272E sidebar
  • #C2C6D0 text

Daylight

Settings: Light What System resolves to in the day

Not the dark palette inverted. A light theme wants more contrast in the text and less in the surfaces, or every panel edge shouts — so the greys sit close together and the colours do the separating. The editor is the one white surface on the screen, which is what makes it the thing you look at.

  • #F7F8FA window
  • #FFFFFF editor
  • #2B2D30 text
  • #0033B3 keyword
  • #00627A call
  • #067D17 string
  • #7A3E9D type
  • #CBDEFB selection

System

The fourth choice is not a palette but a deferral: System follows the Mac's own light and dark setting and resolves to daylight or dusk. It changes while the app is running, so a machine that switches at sunset switches the editor with it.

Abydos is not in that pair on purpose. It is a palette you choose rather than one you are given, and a window that turned amber by itself at six in the evening would be a surprise rather than a preference.
